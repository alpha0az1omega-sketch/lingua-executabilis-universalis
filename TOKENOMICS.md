# L.E.U. TOKENOMICS & ECONOMY

## 1. The Principle of Utility
L.E.U. does not rely on a speculative "shitcoin". Its economy is based on **utility and proof of work**. You pay for the computation, the verification, and the access to the oracle.

## 2. The Genesis Pass (NFT)
- **Total Supply:** 1000 (Strictly limited).
- **Price:** 0.05 ETH (Fixed).
- **Revenue:** 50 ETH total. 
- **Utility:** Lifetime API access, unlimited generation credits, and DAO voting rights.

## 3. API Credits (B2B / B2C)
- **Free Tier:** 10 generations per day (for students and explorers).
- **Pro Tier:** Subscriptions (e.g., 10 EUR/month) for researchers needing unlimited exports and 3D visual artifacts.
- **Enterprise Tier:** Custom licensing for studios/games wanting to use the Genesis Engine offline.

## 4. Treasury Management
All funds generated (NFT sales, API subscriptions) are sent to a **Gnosis Safe multi-signature wallet**.
- **40%:** Development and server costs (Cloudflare, GPU, RPC nodes).
- **30%:** Community bounties (rewards for quests, bug fixes, new molecule models).
- **20%:** Academic partnerships (funding research papers on verifiable AI).
- **10%:** Founder compensation.

## 5. The Deflationary Mechanism
Whenever an artifact is minted on-chain, 1% of the gas fee is burned, slowly reducing the supply of the ecosystem's utility tokens and aligning the incentives of the network.
