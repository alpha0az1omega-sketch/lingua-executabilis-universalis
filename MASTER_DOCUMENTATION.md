## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

L.E.U. is a universal, executable, sonorous, and cryptographic language built on 20 semantic primitives. It is designed to be read, written, executed, and verified by humans, AI agents, and software. Every artifact generated by L.E.U. is cryptographically sealed (SHA-256 + OpenTimestamps) and carries its own proof of existence.

The project has evolved from a formal language into a complete digital ecosystem: a compiler, an interactive 3D universe, an AI tutor, a library of morphologies (particles, atoms, molecules, animals, humans, networks, concepts, plants), and a roadmap toward infinite procedural generation.

## 2. PHILOSOPHY & VISION

**Vision**: Create a digital universe where every entity (physical, biological, conceptual) is measurable, calculable, and cryptographically provable.

**Principles**:
- **Verifiability over trust**: every artifact carries its own proof.
- **Universality**: 20 primitives cover logic, physics, biology, cognition, and value.
- **Accessibility**: human-readable, machine-executable, audibly perceivable.
- **Evolution**: the universe grows through genetic algorithms and procedural generation.
- **Openness**: source code on GitHub, DOI on Zenodo, license on PolyForm.
