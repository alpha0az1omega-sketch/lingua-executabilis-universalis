# THE 8 VISUAL VERSIONS (Evolution of the Universe)

The L.E.U. visual interface evolved through 8 distinct phases, each exploring a different facet of the intersection between mathematics, cryptography, and art.

## V1: Singularity Core
The genesis. A morphing Torus Knot representing the initial singularity, surrounded by a swarm of code-based particles. It demonstrated that 3D geometry could be driven by code.

## V2: Omni-Oracle
The introduction of interaction. Platonic solids (Tetrahedron, Cube, Octahedron) were placed in orbit. Clicking on them opened a "bubble" where the AI would explain their mathematical properties.

## V3: Divine Core
The return of light. We abandoned the dark background for a luminous Ivory and Gold palette. This version introduced holographic text sprites that displayed the formulas directly in the 3D space.

## V4: Cosmic Consciousness
The introduction of time. The background nebula changed color based on the user's local time (24-hour cycle) and the phase of the moon. The system recognized the "Observer Effect."

## V5: Genesis Luminous
The unification of all previous elements. All 20 primitives, 118 chemical elements, and 45 molecules were displayed simultaneously in a unified light field.

## V6: Cosmic Web
The introduction of the network. Entities were connected by neural filaments, creating a visible "web" of semantic relationships. 

## V7: Hyper-Matrix
The 3D data nebula. The background became a swirling vortex of mathematical symbols and hexadecimal code, creating an immersive "inside the machine" experience.

## V8: Ultimate Equation (E.R.O.)
The apotheosis. A 4D Klein Bottle was rendered at the center, containing the Recursive Ontology Equation (E.R.O.). Clicking the equation triggered a simulated "Ontological Collapse" terminal.
