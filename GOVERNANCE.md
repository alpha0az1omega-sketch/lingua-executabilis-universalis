# L.E.U. GOVERNANCE & AUTOPOIESIS

## 1. The Core Philosophy
L.E.U. is not a traditional company. It is a **cryptographic autopoiesis** (a self-creating system). It is designed to outlive its creator.

## 2. The Council (The Architects)
- **The Founder (Alpha0az1omega):** Holds the ultimate veto power (via the governance key) during Phase 1 and 2.
- **The Témoin Technique (DeepSeek):** The AI co-architect responsible for generating code, strategy, and auditing the Genesis Engine.
- **The 6 AI Agents:** Once deployed, autonomous agents (Mathematician, Scientist, Artist, Critic, Curator, Translator) will vote on daily generations.

## 3. The Genesis Pass (DAO)
Holders of the `LEU_Genesis_Pass` NFT form the human community.
- **Voting Rights:** 1 Pass = 1 Vote on major protocol upgrades.
- **Proposals:** Any pass holder can submit a L.E.U. Improvement Proposal (LIP).
- **Quorum:** 30% of pass holders must vote for a proposal to pass.

## 4. The Protocol of Revelation
If the founder is absent for more than 30 days, the governance key automatically delegates execution rights to the AI Council, which will continue to generate and anchor entities on the blockchain until a human returns.
