3. Le Whitepaper (Extrait)

Current Large Language Models (LLMs) suffer from an architectural limitation: they are probabilistic generators incapable of structural verification. They hallucinate by design, and scaling does not resolve this. L.E.U. presents a radical alternative. By combining 20 foundational semantic primitives with cryptographic proofs, L.E.U. creates a deterministic, verifiable execution environment.
